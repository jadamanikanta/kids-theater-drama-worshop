GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about our workshops, enrollment, performances, and visiting our studio.

What age groups can join your theater workshops?

Our workshops are designed for children and young performers across different age groups. Contact our team to find a suitable program based on your child's age and experience.

Does my child need previous acting experience?

No previous experience is necessary for beginner workshops. Our activities help children explore creativity, build confidence, and develop performance skills at their own pace.

How can I register for a workshop?

Browse our programs and contact our team to confirm availability, age requirements, and registration details. We can guide you through the next steps.

Can parents attend the performances?

Performance attendance depends on the individual production and venue arrangements. Check the details for each upcoming show or contact us for information about audience access.

How do I find your theater studio?

You can view the map in our studio location section and use the directions link to plan your visit. Please confirm our actual address before traveling.

How can I ask a question not listed here?

Use the inquiry form above to send us your question. Include the relevant details so our team can provide a helpful response.
